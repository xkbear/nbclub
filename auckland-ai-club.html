奥克兰 AI 俱乐部

在奥克兰，把 AI 从话题变成实践。

NewBee AI Club（先蜂AI俱乐部、先蜂会）是以奥克兰为主要活动基地的新西兰华人 AI 学习与实践社群。我们通过主题 Keynote、无需代码经验也可参与的实操 Workshop、案例交流和小型共创，帮助参与者在真实工作与生活场景中理解 AI。

活动地点会根据主题安排，过往活动曾在 Icehouse Ventures HQ 及 Albany 的 NEXT Park 举办；线上活动会在活动页中明确标注。

Local FAQ

Before you attend.

Where does NewBee AI Club meet in Auckland?

Locations vary by event. Past sessions have been held at Icehouse Ventures HQ and NEXT Park in Albany. Always check the individual event record or announcement.

Do I need coding experience?

Not for every event. Some workshops are explicitly designed for people without coding experience, while keynote talks welcome participants from many professional backgrounds.

Are events available online?

Yes, selected sessions are online. The event archive clearly labels online and Auckland-based activities.

How do I find the latest event?

Visit the official activity calendar. It is the canonical list of NewBee AI Club event records.
