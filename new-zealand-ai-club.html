FAQ

Common questions.

What is NewBee AI Club in New Zealand?

NewBee AI Club, also known as 先蜂AI俱乐部 or 先蜂会, is an independently organised, non-profit Chinese-speaking AI learning and practice community in New Zealand.

Does NewBee AI Club run online sessions?

Yes. NewBee AI Club runs both in-person activities and online workshops or exchanges that can be joined from across New Zealand.

What activities does NewBee AI Club organise?

Keynotes, hands-on AI workshops, member days in real innovation ecosystems, reading and discussion sessions, case reviews, tool exchange and small collaborative experiments.

How can I contact NewBee AI Club?

Email office@new-bee.club for current membership, event or collaboration enquiries.
